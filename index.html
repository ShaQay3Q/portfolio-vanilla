About

I'm Sha - short for Shaghayegh, since people tend to butcher it. I'm a web developer based in Leipzig.

Before this, I studied physics, spent years in hospitality, and co-founded and ran a café/space here built around inclusivity. I'm also a political activist and a semi-professional baker. What ties it all together: I love building something functional out of basic pieces to achieve a cause - a campaign, an elaborate cake, a piece of working code. My background built in me the habit of breaking a problem down to its fundamentals, and of watching how people actually move through a space - what confuses them, what makes them stay. Both show up in how I build for the web.

I work across the stack, dipping my curiosity into different languages, concepts, and technologies - chasing structure and logic as much as interface, with UX instincts baked in rather than bolted on. I care about content and navigation making sense to real people, not just looking clean. I get into the technical details when a project needs it, and I'm just as comfortable telling a client what's outside my scope, so we build something that actually fits.
